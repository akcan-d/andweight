## App name

Measured: Easy Weight Tracker

## Short description

Track your weight and find your real maintenance calories in 2 minutes a day.

## Full description

Measured works out how many calories your body actually burns, without logging every meal.

Most calorie calculators guess your maintenance calories (TDEE) from a formula. Measured works it out from what actually happens to your weight. Each day you log your weight and roughly how much you ate. Each week the app compares the two and adjusts your estimate. The longer you use it, the more it reflects your own body.

A 2-MINUTE DAILY CHECK-IN
• Log today's weight and how much you ate yesterday
• No food diary or barcode scanning: choose from "much less than usual" to "much more than usual"
• Prefer exact numbers? Switch to exact-calorie entry at any time
• Weigh in at night instead? Change the check-in to cover today's meals

AN ESTIMATE THAT ADAPTS TO YOU
• Your maintenance-calorie estimate is updated every week from your real results
• A rolling average smooths out water-weight swings and the odd missed day
• A daily calorie target based on the weekly pace you choose for losing, maintaining or gaining
• A built-in minimum calorie floor, however aggressive your goal

UNDERSTAND YOUR PROGRESS
• Weekly history and trend charts
• Plateau detection that tells a real stall apart from a quiet week
• Milestones that mark your progress along the way
• Optional daily reminders

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• No account or sign-up
• No ads and no analytics
• Everything is stored on your device
• Export and restore your full history with a backup file
• Optional Health Connect sync: share logged weights, or import readings from a smart scale's app

Measured is a tracking tool, not medical advice. If you have a health condition or are planning a major change to your diet, talk to a doctor first.

SUPPORT DEVELOPMENT
Everything above is free, with no time limit. An optional one-time Supporter upgrade adds a few extras:
• Extra color themes
• Bar, area and smoothed-line chart styles
• A choice of share-card designs
