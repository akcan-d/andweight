Measured Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Short version: Measured does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data anywhere. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

What data the app uses

Measured asks you for things like your weight, how much you ate, and optional profile details (sex, height, age, activity level, body fat percentage) so it can estimate your calorie needs. All of this is saved only in a local database on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server - the app has no backend to send data to, and does not use the internet at all.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

Measured the app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting services, and no third-party tracking of any kind. Nothing about your usage of the app is ever observed, recorded, or shared with anyone, including the developer.

This web page is the one exception: it uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly visit counter, just to see how many people read it. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't collect any personal data, and isn't connected to the app in any way.

Backup and sharing features

The app includes an optional backup/restore feature and an optional "share progress" image. Both are generated entirely on your device. A backup file is only ever saved to storage you choose, and a shared image is only ever sent through Android's own system share sheet to an app or contact you pick - Measured itself never uploads either one anywhere.

Permissions the app requests

  • Notifications - only used for the optional daily check-in reminder you can turn on in Settings. No notification is ever sent off your device.
  • Run at device startup - used only to re-arm that same reminder after you restart your phone, if you've turned it on.
  • Ignore battery optimizations - only requested if you explicitly tap a button in Settings asking to make the reminder more reliable on phones with aggressive battery management. It does not run in the background otherwise.
  • Health Connect (read/write weight) - only requested if you tap "Connect to Health Connect" in Settings. See below.

Health Connect

Measured can optionally connect to Health Connect, Android's on-device health data broker, from Settings. This is off by default and nothing is requested unless you turn it on yourself. When connected:

  • If you turn on "Share logged weight automatically", every weight you log in Measured is also written to Health Connect, so any other app you've separately granted read access to (through Health Connect's own permission screen) can see it.
  • Tapping "Import weight readings now" reads recent weight entries already in Health Connect - for example from a smart scale's own app - and fills in any day in Measured that doesn't already have a weight logged, without overwriting a day you've entered by hand.

Either direction can be turned off, and the underlying permission revoked, at any time from Health Connect's own app permissions screen. As with everything else in this policy, none of this involves Measured's own servers, because it doesn't have any - data moves directly between Measured and Health Connect on your device.

Children's privacy

Measured is not directed at children and asks people to confirm they are 18 or older during setup.
